About

I'm a hands-on engineer and student with a passion for building things that work — from bare-metal hardware through to polished software. My work spans Linux systems, mobile development, embedded electronics, and competitive EV engineering.

I'm most comfortable where precision matters: reproducible builds, clean interfaces, and systems that behave exactly as specified. My academic strengths are in Mathematics and Science, and I'm working toward studying Aerospace Engineering at university.

Outside of screens I stay active in endurance sport — triathlon and field hockey — which keeps me disciplined about incremental improvement and long-term goals.

Hardware · Computer Vision

Low-Budget Photogrammetry Scanner

A custom 3D scanner built on the OpenScan platform using a Raspberry Pi, stepper motors, and interchangeable camera modules. Evaluating IR ToF sensors (VL53L5CX / L8CX) and global shutter modules (OV9281, IMX219 NoIR) for high-accuracy point cloud capture at a fraction of commercial cost.

Raspberry Pi OpenScan Python Stepper Motors VL53L5CX OV9281
2025

Linux · GPU / Compute

End-to-end debugging and configuration to run DaVinci Resolve with full GPU acceleration on an AMD Radeon RX 6600 under CachyOS/Arch Linux. Resolved ROCm 7.2 compatibility issues, mesa-git conflicts, bundled library ABI mismatches, and Intel OneAPI TBB interference.

Arch Linux ROCm 7.2 AMD RX 6600 mesa-git DaVinci Resolve Bash
2025
