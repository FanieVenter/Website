01 — About

Who I am

I'm a hands-on engineer and student with a passion for building things that work — from bare-metal hardware through to polished user-facing software. My background spans Linux systems, mobile development, embedded electronics, and competitive EV engineering.

I gravitate toward problems where precision matters: reproducible builds, clean interfaces, and systems that behave exactly as designed. My academic strengths are in Mathematics and Science, and I'm working toward studying Aerospace Engineering at university.

Outside of engineering I stay active in endurance sport — triathlon and field hockey — which keeps me disciplined about incremental improvement and long-term thinking.
